Who am I ?

I'm Tom Demagny, a French engineering student pursuing a double degree in Mathematical Engineering and Data Science at Polytech Clermont and Artificial Intelligence at Université du Québec à Chicoutimi (UQAC), Canada.

I'm passionate about programming, mathematical modeling, and low-level technologies. My experience spans C/C++, OpenGL, Python, and machine learning, with personal projects ranging from fractal exploration and real-time rendering to physics simulations and embedded systems.

I like building things from scratch and understanding what happens beneath the surface. Whether I'm writing a rendering engine, experimenting with fractals, connecting hardware to software, or exploring cryptography, I enjoy the process of figuring things out and turning ideas into something tangible.

For me, programming is as much about curiosity and experimentation as it is about solving problems. I'm always looking for something new to understand, build, or break apart.
